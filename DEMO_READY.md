# Operations demo

Without models or footage, run ./run_demo.sh or run_demo.bat. docs/PITCH_DEMO.md
is the three-minute run-through.

With them, start with .venv-app/bin/python scripts/start_local_workspace.py and open
http://127.0.0.1:8766. See README.md and docs/OPERATIONS.md for tracking,
investigations, reports and local schedules. Current validation is recorded in
docs/DEMO_VALIDATION.md. Model/data limits remain in docs/DATA_SOURCES.md.
