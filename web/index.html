Export geometry

Label this camera

  1. Pause on a clear frame and open Geometry.
  2. Choose Shelf approach area, then Draw on frame. Click around the floor strip in front of the shelf. A person's feet must enter the area.
  3. Click Finish polygon. Rename it, for example “Drinks”. Drag the corner handles to adjust. Avoid overlapping areas.
  4. Draw a doorway line only where a door is visible, then say whether the arrow means entering or leaving. Add checkout and exit areas where visible.
  5. Save geometry, then Start live analysis. Stop live analysis before editing.

Investigate tracks and signals

Open Track explorer to inspect an anonymous ID, its observed intervals and area visit ledger. Click a person box to focus its trail. Download an annotated source frame or trajectory CSV. In Investigations, inspect evidence and save an accepted, dismissed or pending review with notes.

Check pickups against the till

Every pickup candidate is followed to the checkout, the door or the end of its track. This needs a checkout area and a doorway line in the same camera. The result sets a review priority. It does not prove theft or payment.

Generate a shift or monthly report

Set a recording start time in Camera setup when you know it. In Report centre, select recordings and a collection, shift, day, month or custom period, preview coverage, then generate PDF, Word, CSV and JSON. Undated footage is available in collection reports. Add local report schedules for shift end, daily midnight or month end. Schedules run while the app is running.

Understand your results

Tracks are anonymous within one camera run. Visits count entries into areas. Observed dwell adds intervals no more than one second apart. Pickup candidates are model predictions, not confirmed purchases or theft.

Live analysis runs inference on a recording at its source cadence. The detector samples at 5 fps; pickup scoring needs 13 observed samples. Timing shows processing speed and delay. Independent recordings cannot establish cross-camera identities or payment.

Heatmaps show the density of observed footpoint samples in this recording, in camera image coordinates. Traffic charts show distinct tracks per time interval, not entrance counts or store opening hours.
